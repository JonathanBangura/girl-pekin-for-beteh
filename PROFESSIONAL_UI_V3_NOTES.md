# Professional UI V3 — Green + Warm Gold

## Direction
V3 keeps the institutional structure and operational discipline from Professional V2, but gives the public website more energy and visual confidence.

Reference inspiration was used only for design principles: stronger green surfaces, confident warm-gold accents, image-led hierarchy, bolder CTA treatment, alternating section backgrounds, and more visible content segmentation. No template code or layout was copied.

## Core palette
- Deep green: `#0D5F50`
- Dark green: `#073F35` / `#082F29`
- Warm gold: `#F2AA00`
- Gold hover: `#FFC032`
- Warm hero surface: `#FFFAF0`
- Dark text: `#10251F`

## Main public-site changes
- Dark-green public navigation with a confident warm-gold Vote CTA.
- Warm editorial hero background with gold edge accent and stronger foundation-focused headline.
- Featured 2026 award artwork retained and given an offset gold graphic treatment.
- Gold participation strip under the hero.
- New “What We Do” three-card editorial block with strong green/gold/dark visual panels ready for approved photography later.
- Featured award section converted into a full-width deep-green band with gold CTAs and ceremony facts.
- Nominee cards and event cards receive stronger accent treatment while staying professional.
- New dark participation band: Discover / Meet Nominees / Vote / Attend.
- Closing CTA and footer strengthened with deep green and warm gold.
- Public inner-page heroes now use the warm editorial background with a gold edge accent.
- Programme list gets stronger green/gold/dark number panels for clearer rhythm.

## Operational areas
Admin, nominee portal and scanner remain restrained and software-like. Only primary/accent colors were refreshed so they stay connected to the public brand without becoming visually noisy.

## Photography
No stock or AI-generated people were inserted and presented as foundation beneficiaries. The new public cards include clear photography-ready visual regions that can be replaced with approved real foundation images when supplied.
